# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Everyone participating in the LealFinance community is expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone else's
private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make a community
member feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that
violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

The repository does not currently publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting
channel. Do not put sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Contact a
maintainer through a private channel they have provided; security
vulnerabilities must instead follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is
representing the project in public spaces.
